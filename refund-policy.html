Refund & Cancellation Policy

Our policy on cancelling orders, and returns, replacements and refunds.

Back to Home

Last updated: August 2026

1. Overview

This Refund & Cancellation Policy applies to all orders placed with Organic Pesticide - Krishna Worldwide ("we", "our", "us") through our website. We take care to deliver high-quality natural pesticides, bio-gas fertilizers and related agricultural products.

2. Order Cancellation

  • You may cancel an order before it is shipped by contacting us.
  • Once an order has been shipped, it cannot be cancelled, but may be eligible for a return/replacement as described below.
  • For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders cancelled before dispatch, no charge is applicable.

3. Returns and Replacements

We accept returns or provide replacements in the following cases:

  • Damaged / defective product: Product arrived damaged, broken or defective at the time of delivery.
  • Wrong product: You received a product different from what you ordered.
  • Missing items: Part of your order was not delivered.

To be eligible, you must notify us within 7 days of delivery, provide your order ID and, where possible, a photo of the product and packaging.

4. How to Request a Return or Refund

  • Contact us via the website contact form, email, or WhatsApp with your order ID and details.
  • Our team will review your request within 24-48 hours.
  • If approved, we will arrange pick-up/replacement or initiate a refund to the original payment method.

5. Refund Timelines

Once a refund is approved and initiated:

  • Online payments (Razorpay / Paytm): Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5-10 business days, depending on your bank.
  • Cash on Delivery: Refunds are issued by bank transfer / UPI after verification, within 5-10 business days.

6. Non-Returnable Cases

Refunds will not be provided in the following cases:

  • Products used, opened, or damaged after delivery by the customer.
  • Claims made after 7 days of delivery.
  • Not applicable if quality issues were caused by incorrect storage or misuse.

7. Failed / Reversed Payments

If a payment was deducted but the order was not placed successfully, the amount will be auto-reversed or refunded by the payment gateway within 5-10 business days. Please contact us if you do not receive the refund within this period.

8. Contact Us

For any cancellation, return or refund queries, please contact us:
