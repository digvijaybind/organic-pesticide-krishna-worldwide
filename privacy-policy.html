Privacy Policy

How Organic Pesticide - Krishna Worldwide collects, uses and protects your information.

Back to Home

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Organic Pesticide - Krishna Worldwide ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of our customers, dealers and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Personal details such as your name, phone number and email address.
  • Shipping and billing address, including PIN code, city and state.
  • Order and payment details when you make a purchase.
  • Message content and contact information submitted via our contact/enquiry forms.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser information, IP address, and pages visited.
  • Usage data to help us improve our website and services.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders.
  • To verify payments and prevent fraud.
  • To respond to your enquiries and provide customer support.
  • To send order updates, and (with your consent) promotional offers.
  • To improve our products, services and website experience.

4. Payment Data

We use secure third-party payment gateways, including Razorpay and Paytm, to process payments. We do not store your full card numbers, bank account details, or other sensitive payment credentials on our servers. Your payment details are transmitted directly to the payment gateway over encrypted connections and handled under their respective privacy and security policies.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies and local storage to remember your cart contents and preferences so you have a smoother shopping experience. You may disable cookies in your browser settings; however, some features may not function optimally.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only where necessary to provide our services, including with:

  • Payment gateways (Razorpay, Paytm) to process transactions.
  • Logistics and delivery partners to fulfil your orders.
  • Legal or regulatory authorities, where required by law.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal and order information only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, comply with legal and accounting obligations, and resolve disputes.

9. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. You may also object to or restrict certain processing activities. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 18.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
